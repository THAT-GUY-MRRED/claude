================================================================================
 RENAULT RS27-2012 V8  --  the engine in the Lotus E20 (2012 Formula One)
 Compiled 2026-09-30
================================================================================

NOTE ON THE NAME
  "E20" is the Lotus F1 Team chassis (2012 season, Raikkonen / Grosjean).
  The engine in it is the Renault Sport F1 RS27-2012, a 2.4 L 90-degree V8.
  The same engine powered the Red Bull RB8, Williams FW34 and Caterham CT01
  in 2012. The RS27 family was raced from 2007 to 2013 and is derived from
  the 2006 RS26 (Renault's first V8, itself derived from the RS25 V10).

  Designed at Viry-Chatillon (Renault Sport F1, near Paris).
  Assembled by Mecachrome, Aubigny-sur-Nere, France.

  Legend:  [PUB] = published by Renault
           [REG] = mandated by the FIA 2012 technical regulations
           [CALC] = calculated by me from the published/regulated figures
           [EST] = estimate / typical for the era. NOT confirmed by Renault

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HEADLINE SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Designation ............ Renault RS27-2012                          [PUB]
  Type ................... 4-stroke, reciprocating piston, naturally aspirated
  Layout ................. V8, 90 degree bank angle                   [PUB/REG]
  Cylinders .............. 8                                          [PUB]
  Valves ................. 32 (2 inlet + 2 exhaust per cyl), poppet   [PUB/REG]
  Displacement ........... 2400 cc (300 cc per cylinder)              [PUB/REG]
  Max RPM ................ 18,000 rpm (FIA limit from 2009;
                           19,000 in 2007-08, unlimited in 2006)      [PUB/REG]
  Power .................. >750 bhp (typical car installation, typical
                           temperature/pressure/humidity)             [PUB]
  Torque at peak power ... ~297 Nm at 18,000 rpm (750 bhp)            [CALC]
  Specific output ........ ~312 bhp/litre                             [CALC]
  Weight ................. 95 kg (the regulation minimum; Renault ran
                           at exactly the minimum with <1 kg ballast) [PUB/REG]
  Parts count ............ ~2,500 parts                               [PUB]
  Mounting ............... Mid-mounted, fully stressed chassis member
  Clean running range .... from under 4,000 rpm to the limiter        [PUB]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GEOMETRY / DIMENSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cylinder bore .......... max 98 mm                                  [REG]
  Cylinder spacing ....... 106.5 mm (+/- 0.2 mm), fixed               [REG]
  Crank centreline ....... at least 58 mm above the reference plane   [REG]
  Engine CoG height ...... at least 165 mm above the reference plane  [REG]
  Engine CoG long./lat. .. within +/- 50 mm of the engine's geometric
                           centre                                     [REG]

  Bore x stroke (Renault never published these):
    If the full 98 mm bore is used, stroke = 39.8 mm                  [CALC]
    (300 cc / (pi/4 x 9.8^2 cm) = 3.977 cm). All the V8 makers are
    believed to have run at or near 98 mm. Bore/stroke ratio ~2.46.   [EST]
    WARNING: some websites list "66 x 54.6 mm". That is wrong. It
    only gives ~1.5 L.

  Mean piston speed at 18,000 rpm (39.8 mm stroke) ... ~23.9 m/s     [CALC]
  Piston mass ........... 250 g                                       [PUB]
  Piston loading ........ >8,000 g; ~2 tonnes on piston + rod at
                          18,000 rpm (300 rev/s)                      [PUB]

  Crankshaft type ....... Flat-plane (180 deg), as used by all the F1
                          V8s                                         [EST]
  Firing order .......... Not published. Renault simulated 2-3
                          candidate firing orders for torsional vibration
                          and intake acoustics and picked the best    [PUB]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MATERIALS & CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Engine block ........... Aluminium alloy                            [PUB]
  Cylinder heads ......... Cast or wrought aluminium alloy            [REG]
  Pistons ................ Aluminium alloy                            [PUB]
                           (must be Al-Si, Al-Cu, Al-Mg or Al-Zn)     [REG]
  Piston pins ............ Iron-based alloy, one piece                [REG]
  Connecting rods ........ Titanium alloy, machined from one piece.
                           Only the bolted big-end cap and an
                           interference small-end bush are allowed    [PUB/REG]
  Crankshaft ............. Nitrided alloy steel with tungsten-alloy
                           counterweights (the rules cap material
                           density at 19,000 kg/m3). No welding
                           between the front and rear mains           [PUB/REG]
  Camshafts .............. Iron-based alloy, one piece with lobes     [REG]
  Valves ................. Fe, Ni, Co or Ti based alloy. Sodium- or
                           lithium-cooled hollow valves permitted     [REG]
  Valve springs .......... Pneumatic (compressed-air) valve return.
                           Pressure controlled by a passive mechanical
                           regulator or by the ECU                    [REG]
  Timing gears ........... Iron-based alloy (gear-driven cams)        [REG]
  Fasteners .............. Co, Fe or Ni based alloy only             [REG]
  Coatings ............... Free, but no more than 25% of the section
                           thickness and never over 0.8 mm. Renault
                           developed a carbon (DLC-type) low-friction
                           coating for the V8                         [REG/PUB]
  BANNED anywhere on the engine: magnesium alloys, metal matrix
    composites, intermetallics, and alloys with >5% Be, Ir or Re.
    No carbon/composite blocks, heads or pistons.                     [REG]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INDUCTION / FUEL / IGNITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Throttle ............... 8 individual butterflies (one per cylinder)
                           driven by a single actuator                [PUB/REG]
  Intake ................. Fixed-length trumpets (no variable geometry
                           intake, exhaust, valve timing or valve lift) [REG]
  Injection .............. 1 injector per cylinder, into the side or top
                           of the inlet port (port injection, not direct)
                           Max fuel pressure 100 bar (Renault ran 100
                           bar from mid-2006)                         [REG/PUB]
  Ignition ............... High-energy inductive. One coil + one plug per
                           cylinder                                   [PUB/REG]
  Spark plugs ............ Semi-surface discharge                     [PUB]
  Intake air ............. No intake-air cooling devices. No spraying
                           anything into the intake except fuel and sump
                           breather gases                             [REG]
  Fuel / oil ............. Total (race fuel to FIA spec, Total lubricants)
  Fuel used per race ..... ~220 litres (no refuelling from 2010)      [PUB]
  Exhaust gas temp ....... up to ~900 C                               [PUB]
  Exhaust ................ Max 2 exits, rear-facing tailpipes. 2012 rules
                           fixed the exit position and banned
                           off-throttle blowing                       [REG]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LUBRICATION / COOLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Oil system ............. Dry sump with multi-stage scavenging (layout
                           carried over from the RS25 V10). Renault's
                           focus: minimum oil flow and good scavenging to
                           cut friction and windage                   [PUB]
  Piston cooling ......... Oil jets (standard practice for the era)   [EST]
  Sump breather .......... Must vent into the engine air intake       [REG]
  Coolant header tank .... FIA relief valve, max 3.75 bar gauge       [REG]
  Cooling ................ Must not deliberately use latent heat of
                           vaporisation (no evaporative cooling)      [REG]
  Auxiliaries ............ Oil and water pumps, alternator etc. must be
                           mechanically driven from the engine at a fixed
                           ratio to the crank (electric fuel pumps
                           excepted)                                  [REG]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ELECTRONICS / CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ECU .................... McLaren Electronic Systems standard ECU (TAG-310
                           family), mandatory from 2008                [PUB]
  System voltage ......... 12 V nominal; primary regulated voltage max
                           17.0 V DC                                  [REG]
  Throttle response ...... max 50 ms from pedal input to torque demand
                           being met                                  [REG]
  Idle target ............ max 5,000 rpm                              [REG]
  Pedal map .............. only 2 allowed: dry tyres / wet-inter tyres [REG]
  Torque map ............. must increase steadily with pedal position.
                           Above 5,000 rpm, gradient no lower than
                           -0.030 Nm/rpm                              [REG]
  Ignition offsets ....... only above 80% throttle and 15,000 rpm, and
                           only to cut cylinder pressure for
                           reliability                                [REG]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. KERS (2012)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Max power in/out ....... 60 kW (~80 bhp)                            [REG]
  Max energy released .... 400 kJ per lap (~6.7 s at full power)      [REG]
  Renault supplied the KERS motor-generator; battery and packaging were
  largely the team's (Lotus). The KERS motor was on the front of the
  engine, driven from the crank.                                      [EST]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. LIFE / USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Engines per driver ..... 8 per season (2009+)                       [REG]
  Design life ............ ~2,500 km validated, up to ~3,000 km per
                           unit (vs ~400-800 km in the V10 era)       [PUB]
  Homologation ........... Spec frozen to the 2006 Japanese GP spec.
                           Changes only for reliability, installation or
                           "housekeeping"                             [REG]
  Production ............. 1,271 RS26/RS27 engines built by late 2013:
                           683 track, 588 dyno. >2 million km run     [PUB]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. DEVELOPMENT HISTORY (RS26 -> RS27)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Oct 2004  RS26 spec issued. Used as many RS25 V10 parts as possible
            (same scavenging, oil and water circuit concepts)
  2006      RS26: 680 -> 700 bhp over the season. 70-100 piston design
            iterations after crown cracking at high rpm. B-spec at Imola:
            new head, insulated ports, new cams, exhausts. 100 bar
            injection from the French GP
  2007      RS27 homologated and frozen. Rev limit 19,000
  2008      Standard McLaren ECU (no launch control or engine braking aids)
  2009      18,000 rpm limit and 8 engines per season. KERS introduced.
            One-off retune: injectors, trumpets and inlet ports. ~6% better
            fuel efficiency
  2010-11   Exhaust-blown diffusers, off-throttle blowing
  2011-12   Pistons redesigned for longer life. Conrods reinforced.
            Crankshaft layout and installation revised
  2012      Lotus E20 season. Exhaust exit position fixed, off-throttle
            blowing banned
  Total     +50 bhp from the first V8 to the last. +30-40 bhp from 2007.
            58+ wins and 4 drivers' titles (Vettel 2010-2013)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. THINGS THAT ARE NOT PUBLIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Renault never released these, and you won't find them online:
  exact bore/stroke, compression ratio (thought to be ~13-14:1 [EST]),
  valve sizes and included angle, cam profiles and timing, firing
  order, rod length, bearing sizes, port shapes, trumpet lengths,
  exhaust primary lengths, oil/water pump specs, and any drawings or
  CAD. These were trade secrets.

  A realistic path for a home build "inspired by" the RS27:
   - Base the dimensions on the FIA rules (98 mm bore, 106.5 mm spacing,
     90 deg flat-plane V8, 2.4 L)
   - Or use a real production-derived flat-plane V8 as the donor
   - Or buy a real ex-F1 V8. These do come up for sale through
     historic-F1 specialists, but they need specialist rebuilds every
     few hundred km
